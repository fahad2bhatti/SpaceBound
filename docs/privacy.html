SpaceBound: Beyond Earth

Privacy Policy

Effective date: 17 November 2026 · Last updated: 17 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information the Android game SpaceBound: Beyond Earth (the "Game", app ID com.fahadapps.spacebound) uses, and why. The Game is made by Fahad Apps ("we", "us"). If you have any question, email us at fahadsaeed2622@gmail.com.

Short version: we do not ask for your name, email or an account, and we do not run our own servers. Your progress stays on your phone. If you buy something, Google Play handles the payment. If the Game shows ads, Google AdMob serves them and may use your device's advertising ID.

1. Information stored only on your device

The Game saves the following on your phone so you can keep playing. It is not sent to us.

  • Game progress: credits, ships, upgrades, missions, landing scores and badges.
  • Settings: sound, graphics, controls and similar options.
  • Your age group (under 13, 13 to 17, or 18 and over). The Game asks your date of birth once to work this out. Only the age group is kept, never your date of birth.
  • Which version of the Terms and this Policy you accepted, and when.
  • Items you bought, such as premium currency balance, so the Game can show them.

We cannot see or access this information. You can delete it at any time with Settings → Reset progress in the Game, or by uninstalling the app or clearing its data in Android settings.

2. In-app purchases (Google Play Billing)

The Game may offer optional purchases, such as premium currency, ships, bundles or removing ads. All payments are processed by Google Play. We never see or store your card, bank or payment details. Google shares with us only what is needed to deliver your purchase, such as an order ID, the item bought, the purchase time and a purchase token. We use this only to give you the item, to restore it if you reinstall, and to handle refunds and fraud. Google's handling of your payment is covered by the Google Privacy Policy and the Google Payments Privacy Notice.

The store is hidden for players who tell the Game they are under 13.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The Game may show ads, including optional "watch an ad for a reward" videos. Ads are provided by Google AdMob. To show ads, measure them and prevent fraud, AdMob may collect:

  • your device's advertising ID (Android Advertising ID);
  • your IP address and the approximate location it suggests (country or city level);
  • device and app information, such as phone model, Android version and language;
  • ad interactions, such as which ads were shown or tapped, and crash or performance data.

Where the law requires it (for example in the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland), the Game asks for your consent before personalised ads are shown, and you can change your choice later. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in Android settings (Settings → Privacy → Ads, the name may differ on your phone). For players who say they are under 13, ad requests are marked as child-directed, so only non-personalised, family-safe ads are shown. Buying Remove Ads turns off the ads you did not choose to watch.

Learn more in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

4. Sharing a result

If you tap Share after a landing, the Game opens your phone's normal share menu with a short text message, such as your score. Nothing is sent unless you choose an app and send it yourself.

5. What we do not do

  • We do not create accounts or ask for your name, email, phone number, contacts, photos or precise location.
  • We do not sell your personal information.
  • We do not run our own analytics or tracking.

7. Children

The Game is suitable for a general audience. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Players who say they are under 13 are asked to read the Terms with a parent or guardian, do not see the store, and only get child-directed, non-personalised ads. If you are a parent and think your child's information was collected, email us and we will help.

8. How long information is kept

Information on your device is kept until you reset progress, clear the app's data or uninstall the Game. Purchase records are kept by Google Play under Google's own policy. AdMob keeps data according to Google's policies.

9. Security

Your game data never leaves your phone through us. Payments and ads use Google's secure systems. No method is 100% secure, but we keep the information we handle to the minimum.

10. Your choices and rights

You can delete your game data at any time as described in section 1, turn off personalised ads in your ad consent choice or Android settings, and contact us with any privacy request. Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal information. Since we do not hold personal information about you ourselves, most requests are handled by deleting the data on your device or through your Google Account controls.

11. Changes to this Policy

We may update this Policy, for example when the Game gets new features. We will change the date at the top of this page, and if the change is important, the Game will ask you to accept it again.
