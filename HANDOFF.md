# SpaceBound: Beyond Earth - handoff notes (updated 2026-11-24)

## What this is
A 3D browser space-shuttle simulator (Three.js) wrapped as an Android app with Capacitor.
Gameplay: walk the spaceport, buy/select ships, board, countdown + launch, fly to Earth orbit / Moon / planets,
land, explore on foot, return. Progress is saved in localStorage. No server, no auth, no API calls.
The first version was generated by Grok from a long spec prompt (ask Fahad for it if needed).

## Code map
- src/game/engine.ts   - the `Spacebound` class: game loop, screens (menu/play/pause/garage/missions/solar/settings/credits),
                         phases (pad/cockpit/flight/surface...), flight physics, Solar chart, nav target + beacons,
                         `getSnap()` (state for React), `escape()` (public Esc logic), `projectTarget()` (destination marker).
- src/game/input.ts    - keyboard/mouse/touch state. Touch: `input.touch` {x,y,jump,run,rcsUp,rcsDown,pitchUp,pitchDown}.
                         In flight: touch.x<-0.25 = steer left, >0.25 = right; touch.y>0.3 = throttle up, <-0.3 = down.
- src/game/models.ts   - procedural ships/Earth/Moon/stars (`buildStars`).
- src/game/save.ts     - localStorage save.
- src/game/waypoint.ts - pure `placeWaypoint()` for the on-screen destination marker.
- src/components/spacebound/Game.tsx - all React UI: Menu, Panel, Cmd, Hud, Pause, Solar, Touch, Stick, TouchBtn, Waypoint.
  UI talks to the engine with `act((e) => e.something())`.
- src/styles.css       - Tailwind theme (--color-bg #07090D, primary #8ECFFF, accent #E8893A) + compact-menu CSS.
- android/             - Capacitor Android project (MainActivity.java patched for fullscreen).
- make_icons.mjs + assets/ - icon/splash source images (generated with sharp); `npx capacitor-assets generate --android`.

## Contracts: rescue (4A) and EVA station repair (4B)
Branch feature/eva (built on feature/rescue-eva). Contract content lives in `src/game/contracts/data.ts`, behaviour in `objectives.ts`
(`registerObjective`), events in `ContractEventMap` (types.ts). Nothing type-specific is hard-coded in the engine.
- **4A rescue**: objective `rescueCrew` { at, count 1-3 } (E on the marker, only the next crew marker is shown, inventory.crew counts),
  contract `type: "rescue"` relabels the timer "CREW OXYGEN", fail text "The crew ran out of air.", `reward.crewBonus` per crew saved.
  Missions: moon-rescue-solo / moon-rescue-pair / mars-rescue-trio.
- **4B station**: Vesper Station now sits on the Earth-Moon line, `STATION_DIST` (75 su) from Earth, range rating 80 (catalog DESTS.station.route).
  Model is `buildStation()` (primitives). Repair points are station-local `Spot`s with `scene: "eva"` and x/y/z.
- **4B EVA phase** (`phase === "eva"`, engine.ts `// eva-v1` block): from flight, within 9 su of the station and under 8 su/s, prompt "E Start EVA".
  The shuttle is frozen (updateFlight does not run) and EVA has its own controls: W/S thrust, A/D turn, Space up, X down, hold E repair,
  E near the shuttle (2.2 su) = airlock. Phone: stick + E (tap/hold) + UP/DOWN. Camera reuses the Phase 1 free camera via `stepFreeCam()`.
  Oxygen drains in `onFoot || onEva`, multiplied 1x -> 3x between 10 and 20 su from the shuttle (soft limit). HUD: range, arrow to shuttle, hold bar.
- **Objectives**: `repairPoint` { at, seconds, label, bolts? } (hold-to-repair via `manager.hold()`; bolts > 1 = that many holds, each at its own marker),
  `returnToShuttle` (completed by the `evaEnded` event). Hazards: `ContractDef.hazards.debris` floating debris, hit = -8 s oxygen
  (`manager.drainOxygen`). Station alarm lamp blinks red when timer or oxygen is in its last 30 s.
- **EVA contracts** (type "eva-repair"): eva-quick-fix (easy), eva-station-service (normal, 3-bolt panel), eva-debris-storm (hard, 3 debris).
  Flow: reachLocation(station) -> repairPoint(s) -> returnToShuttle -> returnToEarth.
- Save: no schema change. EVA state is not saved; a reload during EVA resumes in flight at the shuttle, the contract (progress, oxygen) is kept.
- Not verified on a device yet: EVA feel and speeds (EVA_ACCEL / EVA_MAX), marker reach (EVA_REACH), crew marker placement on rocks, FPS on phone.

## Landing stars (branch feature/landing-stars)
- `src/game/landing.ts`: `LANDING_CFG` holds every threshold (stars: descent / drift / tilt / center-of-zone, Perfect Landing limits + bonus 150 cr, contract multiplier 1 / 1.15 / 1.3, score weights). `rateLanding()` gives 1-3 stars (assist caps at 2), `gradeLanding()` is unchanged.
- Engine: `resolveGround` measures tilt and zone-center distance, `recordLanding` returns a `LandingResult` shown by `components/spacebound/Landing.tsx` (non-blocking, tap skips, low quality / reduced motion = no animation). The `landed` contract event carries `stars`; `manager.succeed()` multiplies the credits of a contract finished by that landing. Free-roam credits are unchanged (grade bonus + the Perfect bonus).
- Save v3: `best` (stars + score per planet id and zone id) and `badges`; `sanitize()` is the migration (v1/v2 load with empty best). Mission board cards show best stars of their planet.
- Share: `src/game/share.ts` (config: game name + placeholder link). Capacitor Share on the phone (needs `npm i` + `npx cap sync android` once), Web Share in browsers, clipboard fallback. Text only, no share-card image.
- Tests: `npx tsx scripts/landing.test.mts` (stars, score, multiplier, best, migration, share text) and `npx tsx scripts/contracts.test.mts`.

## Sun, gas landing, new ships (merged: PR #10-#12)
- Sun (`buildSun`, `SUN_DIST` 3500, `SUN_RADIUS` 260): visual + hazard. `updateSunHazard()` drains life near it, solar wall stops you, overheat = auto-abort to Earth orbit.
- Gas giants (Jupiter..Neptune) are landable on a cloud deck (`kind: "cloud"` terrain, no marked zones). `BODY_MULT` in landing.ts: 2 / 2.2 / 2.6 / 3.
- Ships pathfinder / nebula / galaxy unlock with `neptune-survey` (garage uses a split layout).
- Up/Down arrows pitch only (+-75 deg, no roll): `stabilizeFlight()`.

## Missions screen + guides (missions-guide-v1, branch feature/missions-guide)
- `src/game/guide.ts`: `guideFor(place)` = per-planet guide (getting there / landing / zones / on foot / dangers). Numbers come from catalog, planets and landing config. `placeOfMission()` groups campaign missions by planet (orbit and station count as Earth).
- `src/components/spacebound/MissionsScreen.tsx` (replaces the old Missions panel in Game.tsx): one side has all details (guide, campaign, contracts), the other side is the planet strip. CSS: `.sb-ms*` in styles.css (same split idea as the garage).
- Contracts: the planet screen lists every visible contract (`manager.allView()`, snap `contracts.all`) and `acceptContract` now uses `accept(id, { any: true })`, so any unlocked contract can be taken from its planet. The 3-slot board still exists in the save/manager but the screen no longer shows it.
- New campaign side missions (end of MISSIONS, so the main chain and old saves are unchanged): jupiter / saturn / uranus / neptune `-landing` (require that planet's flyby or survey), paid from the landing code via `tryEvent(`${body}-landing`)`.
- New contracts (data.ts): jupiter-cloud-survey, saturn-deck-survey (normal), uranus-ice-survey, neptune-deep-survey (hard). Land on the cloud deck, 30-45 s on foot, return to Earth.
- Tests: `npx tsx scripts/missions.test.mts`. Not verified on a phone yet: layout on small landscape, long-trip timers (1500 / 2100 / 3000 / 3600 s), cloud-deck walk with surviveTime.

## Store, billing and ads (merged: PR #17, #18)
- Products are in `src/game/store.ts` (ids must match Play Console exactly): nova_100 / 550 / 1200 / 2600 (consumable),
  starter_pack, remove_ads, commander_bundle (one time). `grantProduct()` never gives the same order twice.
- `src/game/billing.ts`: Play Billing via @capgo/native-purchases. Item is saved first, then consumed / acknowledged.
  `unfinished()` runs at every start, `restorePurchases()` brings back one-time items after a reinstall.
  Purchases are checked on the phone only (no server check of the purchase token, see Known issues).
- `src/game/ads.ts`: AdMob banner (menu only), interstitial (every 2nd mission end, 3 min gap), rewarded (player choice).
  Test ad units unless built with `VITE_ADS_REAL=1`. Under-13 players: no ads, no Store. app-ads.txt is live on fahad2bhatti.github.io.
- Release: `VITE_ADS_REAL=1 npm run android:aab`. `scripts/check-release.mjs` refuses test billing, test ads or a missing upload key.

## Known issues / untested
- Purchases are not verified on a server. A modded APK could fake a purchase. Low priority while the save is local
  (a cheater can also edit the save); add a Cloud Function check of the purchase token if a cloud save or leaderboard is added.
- Nova bought with real money lives only in the local save. It survives a reinstall only through Android Auto Backup
  (`allowBackup="true"`). One-time items come back with Restore purchases.
- Changing the destination mid-flight (MAP button) was not tested against mission/landing rules.
- Phone FPS not measured on low-end phones. Touch screens start on Low graphics.
- `npx tsc --noEmit`: the old Input.override / pressMoved errors are fixed (2026-11-24).

## Ship looks: paint and stickers (looks-v1, branch feature/ship-looks)
- Cosmetic only. The stock look is free and is the default; every other paint and sticker costs Nova and is bought once, then works on every ship the player owns. Nothing changes speed, fuel or range.
- `src/game/cosmetics.ts`: data (`PAINTS`, `STICKERS`, prices) and pure functions (`lookOf`, `lookCost`, `buyAndEquip`, `sanitizeCosmetics`, `sanitizeLooks`). Prices are in this file.
- `src/game/livery.ts`: the 3D part. `applyLook(rig, assets, look)` swaps the ship's main body material and accent material for tinted copies (shared materials are never touched) and puts a decal on each side of the hull, placed by a ray against the hull. Sticker pictures are drawn with canvas once. It never throws; on a problem the ship stays stock.
- `models.ts`: `buildShip(id, assets, look?)` now builds the base ship, then applies the look. The engine passes the look at every call (menu, pad, garage, flight, surface); the flight rig is rebuilt when the look changes (`rigKey`).
- Save: `cosmetics` (bought ids) and `looks` (per ship). Same save version 4, `sanitize()` fills them in for older saves; `keepPaid()` keeps them on new game / reset. A look that uses a part that is not owned falls back to stock.
- Garage UI: `LookPicker` in Game.tsx. Tapping a paint or sticker previews it on the ship (`previewLook`), then Equip / Buy (`confirmLook`) or Undo (`cancelLook`). If Nova is short the button opens the Store Nova tab. Hidden for under-13 players (no Store).
- Tests: `npx tsx scripts/looks.test.mts` (pure logic). Not verified on a phone yet: how the paint looks on each of the 12 ships (the body / accent guess is by material volume), decal position and size (some ships may need a tuned spot), sticker pictures, garage layout on small landscape screens.

## Flight controls, autopilot, mini-map (2026-11-24)
- **Arcade flight** (`arcade-flight-v1`, engine.ts updateFlight): the velocity turns towards the nose at `ALIGN_RATE` (1.8/s), speed kept.
  Off when range-locked or out of fuel. Before this, turning only rotated the nose and the ship kept drifting the old way.
- **Autopilot** (`autopilot-v1`): pure planner in `src/game/autopilot.ts` (no three.js): climb out of a planet, detour round planets / the Sun,
  cruise, brake early near the target, report "arrived". Engine: `toggleAutopilot()` (AUTO button, P key), `apTarget()` (hover 12 su over a
  landing beacon, the flyby beacon for gas giants, the station, the gate), `autopilotStep()` (eases the nose, sets throttle, brakes).
  Any steer / pitch / throttle input hands control back. Landing assist is skipped while it flies. It never lands by itself.
  Tests: `npx tsx scripts/autopilot.test.mts` flies 13 real routes with a copy of the flight model (keep its numbers in step with engine.ts).
- **Mini-map** (`minimap-v1`): `src/components/spacebound/MiniMap.tsx`, canvas, heading-up, target diamond / rim arrow, tap = Solar chart.
  Data from `engine.miniMap()`. The HUD right column is now one flex column (mini-map + target box, landing zones under them).
- **Solar chart**: orange "you are here" marker (`chartShipPos`, placed between the two nearest planets / gates, the orrery is not to scale)
  and a "You: near Mars" line (`shipWhere`).
- **Flyby feedback**: a beacon passed before its previous mission now says which mission must be done first (`blockedText`).
- **Takeoff**: leaving the planet that was the nav target sets the target to Earth (`enterFlightFrom`).
- Not verified on a phone yet: alignment feel (ALIGN_RATE), autopilot turn speed near gas giants, mini-map size on small landscape screens.
